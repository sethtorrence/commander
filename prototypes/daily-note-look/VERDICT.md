# PROTOTYPE verdict: Daily Note look and feel

Answers [Daily Note look and feel](https://github.com/sethtorrence/commander/issues/9) on the Commander wayfinder map. Everything in this folder is throwaway; the decision lives on the ticket.

- **Round 1** (`index.html`): four structures. The author picked **C, the journal stream outliner**.
- **Round 2** (`round-2/index.html`): three futuristic brutalist takes on C, light and dark, with Titanus. The author picked **Industrial**.
- **Round 3** (`round-3/index.html`): a signal colour picker on Industrial. The author picked **phosphor green, #00E676**.

Reference screenshots of the locked look: `round-3/screenshots/phosphor-dark.png` and `round-3/screenshots/phosphor-light.png`. The reference implementation of the locked look is `round-3/industrial.html?signal=00E676&auto=1`.
